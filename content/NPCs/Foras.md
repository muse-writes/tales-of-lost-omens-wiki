Foras is a canon non-player character introduced in Kingmaker. He is a human sorcerer. He is cursed by Yog-Sothoth to exist outside of the standard flow of time, and no longer ages. He was sealed away under Candlemere for for thousands of years before emerging during the events of Kingmaker.
