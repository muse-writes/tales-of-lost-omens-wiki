April is a player character introduced in Twilight Academy. She is a human rogue with a monk dedication. She has long-since forgotten her origins and is the current owner of the haunted Rosetti Manor, outside of Galduria. She is also presently the Aspect of Lust, as determined by the ruins of Allinor.
