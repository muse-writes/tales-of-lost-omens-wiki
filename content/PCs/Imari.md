Imari is a player character originally introduced in Twilight Academy. She is an anxious tripkee alchemist, with a witch archetype and a mysterious psychopomp patron.
## Description
Imari is a young tripkee woman with orange and black patterned skin resembling a poison dart frog. She typically wears leather clothes, including a large pointed witch hat, patterned coat and trousers strapped with alchemical equipment, and boots. She stands at just under 4ft tall, and carries herself with a tense and awkward demeanour.
## Background
Born to two mothers of an urban tribe of tripkees in Senghor, imari grew up around a large number of siblings, cousins, and other relatives. She took an interest in the ritualistic disciplines of witchcraft from a young age, brewing and crafting a number of fascinating concoctions. She first set something on fire at age 4. By her teens she'd lost count. She never really felt particularly like she fit in, and after her mum died, a thirst for adventure drove her to work on a ship headed for Varisia.

In her time at sea, she heard plenty of tales from her fellow sailors. Among them, she heard of the Twilight Academy in Galduria. Upon landing, she found her way there and asked for a place in the school. She found herself lodging with Varrolgu, the local apothecary, helping out at her shop sometimes in return for a space in her loft. Imari began a course in Witchcraft at the academy, gaining a sugar glider familiar she named Issa.

When magic in the town of Varisia stopped functioning properly, Imari's frustration with the spellcasting side of witchcraft drove her to pivot into exclusively working with more practical aspects, becoming more accurately described as an alchemist. It also severed her magical connection to Issa, causing her to become mundane.
