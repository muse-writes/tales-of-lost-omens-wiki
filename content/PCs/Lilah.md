Lilah Julia Andoricus (a.k.a. Lilah "Once-lost") is a player character originally from Abomination Vaults but has also featured as a noteworthy NPC and hireling in Tales of Lost Omens: Sinner's End. She is a human summoner with an eidolon named Prometheus. She is married to Wrin Sivinxi and acts as captain of the Thief's Redoubt, a stolen Chelaxian brig.
## Description
Lilah is a woman of medium height and has a slightly broader than average build. She wears her curly white hair loose, or occasionally gathered into a ponytail. She has amethyst purple eyes. Lilah possesses a number of scars on her body, most notably is the round fist-sized patch in her chest and back, inflicted by a cannonball.

Lilah has an easygoing personality, with a bit of a mischievous streak. She is a font of obscure skills, including cooking and larceny. She picked up many of these skills on shore leave, particularly whilst in the company of her friend Eric, a rogue who always seemed to find her, no matter how far away she seemed to.
## Background
Lilah was born in 4698 AR and raised as a boy in the Chelaxian port of Ostenso, well-known for being the Chelaxian navy's headquarters. She inherited her purple eyes from her mother, who has Azlanti heritage, and white hair from her father who is half-Nidalese.

At some point in her early teenage years, Lilah abandoned her family home and started living as a street urchin. Shortly afterwards she decided to transition, and sought out the difficult-to-find mulibrous tincture for this purpose. She adopted the name "Julia" for this time period.

At the tender age of seventeen, Lilah was abducted by a press-gang and forced at swordpoint to serve upon the Allobula, a Chelaxian naval vessel. She would spend the next six years of her life as a deckhand, during which she saw combat several times. After this she lost her source of mulibrous tincture.

In 4721 AR, Lilah mistakenly killed a fellow crewmember during a battle. Itching for a scapegoat to blame for the Allobula's losses, Lilah was imprisoned and due to be executed. During this imprisonment she was approached for the first time by Prometheus, a guardian spirit and eidolon who had inhabited her family since ancient times. Prometheus manifested, and took the form of a phoenix. Without Lilah really consciously deciding anything, Prometheus had set fire to the Allobula, and the two of them escaped to the nearby Isle of Kortos as the ship sank.
