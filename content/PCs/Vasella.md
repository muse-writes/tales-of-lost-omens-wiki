Vasella Cynosure is a player character who was originally introduced in Twilight Academy. She is a human kineticist with an exemplar dedication. She is an orphaned angelkin, blessed by the missing goddess Lissala. She was taken in at a young age by the wealthy Garrington family, who reside in Magnimar.
